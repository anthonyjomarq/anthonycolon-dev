Privacy Policy

MuscleMap
Last updated: March 26, 2026

Anthony Colon Dominguez ("we," "our," or "us") built MuscleMap as a free application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Data You Provide

MuscleMap allows you to enter the following data to track your fitness progress:

  • Profile information: Name, birth date, height, weight, fitness goals, and experience level
  • Workout data: Exercises performed, sets, reps, weight lifted, workout duration, and notes
  • Body measurements: Weight, body fat percentage, and various body part measurements
  • Personal records: Automatically tracked based on your workout performance

Data Stored Locally

All of the data listed above is stored locally on your device using on-device storage. We do not transmit this data to any external servers in the current version of the app.

Error Reporting

We use Sentry, a third-party error monitoring service, to collect crash reports and diagnostic information. This may include:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version
  • Error logs and stack traces
  • Anonymous session identifiers

Sentry data is used solely to identify and fix bugs. It does not include your workout data, profile information, or body measurements. Learn more at Sentry's Privacy Policy.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your location
  • We do not access your camera or microphone
  • We do not serve advertisements
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties
  • We do not use analytics or tracking services beyond error reporting

Data Storage and Security

Your fitness data is stored exclusively on your device. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, all stored information will be permanently deleted. We recommend keeping your own backups if your data is important to you.

Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time by:

  • Clearing the app's data through your device settings
  • Uninstalling the application

Children's Privacy

MuscleMap is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when they are posted on this page.

Future Features

In future versions, MuscleMap may offer optional cloud sync and backup features. If and when these features are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly. These features will always be opt-in.
